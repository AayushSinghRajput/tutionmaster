# How TuitionMaster Works

> TuitionMaster provides an online platform where students and parents can discover tutors and tutors can create profiles to present their teaching services.

## Overview

TuitionMaster is designed to simplify the process of discovering tutors online.

The platform provides separate experiences for students or parents looking for tutors and tutors who want to present their teaching services.

## For Students and Parents

### 1. Visit TuitionMaster

Visit the official TuitionMaster website:

https://www.tuitionmaster.guru/

### 2. Browse Tutors

Open the tutor directory to explore publicly available tutor profiles:

https://www.tuitionmaster.guru/teachers

### 3. Review Tutor Profiles

A tutor profile may provide information including:

* Name
* Subjects
* Qualifications
* Teaching experience
* Teaching preferences
* Teaching mode
* Location or service area
* Other profile information provided by the tutor

### 4. Find a Suitable Tutor

Students or parents can use the available tutor information to identify tutors who appear relevant to their educational requirements.

The suitability of a tutor should be evaluated based on the information available on the tutor's profile and any further communication between the student or parent and the tutor.

## For Tutors

### 1. Register

Tutors can register on TuitionMaster to create an account and begin creating their tutor profile.

### 2. Create a Profile

Tutors can provide relevant information about themselves and their teaching services.

A profile may include information such as:

* Qualifications
* Teaching experience
* Subjects taught
* Teaching preferences
* Teaching mode
* Location or service area
* Profile description

### 3. Present Teaching Services

Once the profile is created and published according to TuitionMaster's platform processes, students and parents can discover the tutor through the platform.

### 4. Connect With Students

Students or parents can use the available platform functionality to proceed with a tutor they are interested in.

## Important Information

* TuitionMaster provides the platform for discovering and presenting tutor information.
* Tutor profiles are created using information provided by the respective tutors.
* Tutor information can change over time.
* Do not assume information that is not explicitly stated in a tutor's profile.
* Students and parents should independently evaluate whether a tutor is appropriate for their requirements.
