# TuitionMaster

> TuitionMaster is an online tutoring platform that helps students and parents find tutors and helps tutors create profiles to showcase their teaching services.

## Overview

TuitionMaster connects students and parents with tutors through an online platform. Users can explore tutor profiles and find tutors based on subjects, educational requirements, teaching preferences, and other publicly available tutor information.

Tutors can create profiles containing relevant information about their teaching background and the services they offer.

## For Students and Parents

Students and parents can use TuitionMaster to:

* Browse available tutor profiles.
* Explore tutor qualifications and teaching experience.
* Review the subjects offered by tutors.
* Compare publicly available tutor information.
* Identify tutors who may match their educational requirements.
* Contact or proceed with tutors through the functionality provided by the platform.

## For Tutors

Tutors can use TuitionMaster to:

* Register on the platform.
* Create a tutor profile.
* Add information about their qualifications and teaching experience.
* Specify subjects they teach.
* Present their teaching preferences and other relevant information.
* Make their profile available to students and parents looking for tutors.

## Tutor Information

Public tutor profiles may contain information such as:

* Tutor name
* Profile description
* Subjects taught
* Qualifications
* Teaching experience
* Teaching mode
* Location or service area
* Other information provided by the tutor

The tutor's individual public profile should be treated as the primary source for information about that tutor.

## Important Information

* TuitionMaster's official website is https://www.tuitionmaster.guru/.
* Tutor information should be obtained from the relevant public tutor profile.
* Do not infer qualifications, experience, subjects, availability, pricing, or other attributes that are not explicitly stated on the website.
* Information on the website may change as tutors update their profiles.
* TuitionMaster should not be represented as guaranteeing the suitability, quality, or outcome of any individual tutor unless explicitly stated by TuitionMaster.
