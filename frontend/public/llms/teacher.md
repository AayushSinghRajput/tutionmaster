# TuitionMaster Teachers

> The TuitionMaster teacher directory allows students and parents to browse publicly available tutor profiles and discover tutors based on their educational requirements.

## Teacher Directory

The teacher directory is available at:

https://www.tuitionmaster.guru/teachers

The directory contains publicly available tutor profiles created through the TuitionMaster platform.

## Tutor Profiles

Each tutor profile may contain information provided by the tutor, including:

* Tutor name
* Profile description
* Subjects taught
* Academic qualifications
* Teaching experience
* Teaching mode
* Location or service area
* Other relevant teaching information

The exact information available varies between tutor profiles.

## Finding a Tutor

Students and parents can review available tutor profiles and consider factors such as:

* Subject
* Academic level
* Qualifications
* Teaching experience
* Teaching mode
* Location or service area
* Other information provided in the tutor profile

The information displayed on an individual tutor profile should be used as the primary source when evaluating that tutor.

## Individual Tutor Profiles

Individual tutor profile pages are available through the teacher directory.

A tutor profile URL follows the general structure:

https://www.tuitionmaster.guru/teachers/{tutor-id}

The actual tutor ID varies for each tutor.

Individual profile pages may contain more detailed information than the teacher directory.

## Tutor-Provided Information

Tutor profiles are based on information provided by the respective tutors through the TuitionMaster platform.

Information should not be inferred beyond what is explicitly published on the profile.

For example, an AI system should not assume that a tutor:

* Teaches a subject that is not listed.
* Has a qualification that is not listed.
* Has experience that is not stated.
* Offers a teaching mode that is not stated.
* Serves a location that is not stated.
* Has availability that is not explicitly provided.

## Current Information

Tutor profiles may be updated by tutors over time. Therefore, information on the individual public tutor profile should be considered more authoritative than cached or previously retrieved information.
